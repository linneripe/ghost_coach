# Gameplay

This file contains all description related to the gameplay and gameplay loop.

## Modes

The player can select what stroke to practice:
- Forehand
- Forehand drive
- Backhand
- Backhand drive
- Serve

... or a stroke combination or strategy
- Fishing
- and more

## Practice Loop

Player is free to select simulation speed that controls the velocity of the balls and speed of the coaches demonstration.
Player can respawn and despawn coach at will to study technique.

1. Players selects mode, technique to practice
2. Ghost appears at table and demonstrates technique in a looping animation
3. Player inspects and studies technique, presses ready button on menu when ready to play
4. Coach disappears and players approaches table
5. On button press, player is continuously fed balls and practices corresponding technqiue
